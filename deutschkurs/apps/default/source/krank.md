# krank

## Examples

- Mein Kind ist krank und bleibt heute zu Hause. :: My child is ill and stays at home today.
- Ich rufe in der Schule an, weil meine Tochter krank ist. :: I am calling the school because my daughter is ill.

## Notes

At school you *krank melden*: report that the child is ill.
