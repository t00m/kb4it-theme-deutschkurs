# Elternbrief

A letter from the school comes home in the school bag. You have to understand what to do and by when.

## I can

- [ ] Ich kann verstehen, wann und wo der Ausflug ist. :: I can understand when and where the trip is.
- [ ] Ich kann verstehen, was mein Kind mitbringen muss. :: I can understand what my child has to bring.
- [ ] Ich kann der Lehrerin Bescheid sagen, wenn mein Kind krank ist. :: I can let the teacher know when my child is ill.
