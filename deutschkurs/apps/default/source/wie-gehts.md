# Wie geht's?

## Variants

- Wie geht es Ihnen? :: How are you? (formal)
- Gut, danke. Und dir? :: Fine, thanks. And you?

## Usage

Greeting between people who know each other, such as other parents at the school gate. With *Sie*: *Wie geht es Ihnen?*
