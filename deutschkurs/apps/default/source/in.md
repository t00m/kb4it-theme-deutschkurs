# in

## Examples

- Die Kinder sind in der Schule. :: The children are at school.
- Morgen gehen wir in den Park. :: Tomorrow we are going to the park.

## Notes

Where? Dativ: *in der Schule*. Where to? Akkusativ: *in den Park*. *in dem* is shortened to *im*: *im Park*.
